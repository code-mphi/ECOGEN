Transport of water–air mixture

This test case, realised with the Discrete Equation Method and the HLLC Riemann solver, is important since it validates the capacity of the numerical method to treat advection of a mixture between fluids without creating spurious oscillations on pressure or velocity profiles. The equation of state used is stiffened gas with \(\gamma = 1.4\) and \(P_{\infty} = 0\) for air and \(\gamma = 4.4\) and \(P_{\infty} = 6 \times 10^{8}\) for water. Input files for this test are available in ./libTests/referenceTestCases/NEqDEM/1D/transport/waterAirAlphaDiff/. The corresponding uncommented line in ECOGEN.xml is:

<testCase>libTests/referenceTestCases/NEqDEM/1D/transport/waterAirAlphaDiff/</testCase>
\[ \begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|} \hline & \text{High pressure} & \text{High pressure} & \text{Low pressure} & \text{Low pressure} \\ \hline \text{Phase} & \text{Water} & \text{Air} & \text{Water} & \text{Air} \\ \hline \rho \, [\text{kg/m}^3] & 1000 & 100 & 100 & 1 \\ \hline u \, [\text{m/s}] & 20 & 20 & 20 & 20 \\ \hline P \, [\text{Pa}] & 10^{5} & 10^{5} & 10^{5} & 10^{5} \\ \hline \end{array} \] Initial conditions for 1D advection of water-air mixture.

The initial characteristics of the run are:

\begin{array}{|l|l|} \hline \textbf{Characteristic} & \textbf{Value} \\ \hline \text{dimension} & 1 \text{ m} \\ \text{mesh size} & 400 \\ \text{interface position} & 0.4 \text{ m} \\ \text{boundary conditions} & \text{non-reflecting} \\ \text{final solution time} & 2 \times 10^{-2} \ \mathrm{s} \\ \text{solution printing frequency} & 1 \times 10^{-3} \ \mathrm{s} \\ \text{precision} & \text{1st order} \\ \hline \end{array}
