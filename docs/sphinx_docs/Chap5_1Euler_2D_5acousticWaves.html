Acoustic waves

This is a plain pressure field where a source term is used to generate one-way acoustic waves [MC17]. The waves here have a sinusoidal shape. 4 pulses are sent at a frequency of 300 kHz and have a pressure amplitude of 10 kPa. This case is a 2D Cartesian test case. The domain is only composed of water. The source term is located at 10.e-3 m in x and y directions and pulses are directed toward the x direction. This test is referenced in ./libTests/referenceTestCases/euler/2D/acousticWave/planeSinusoidalPulse/. The corresponding uncommented line in ECOGEN.xml is:

<testCase>./libTests/referenceTestCases/euler/2D/acousticWave/planeSinusoidalPulse/</testCase>
