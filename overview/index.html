Overview

ECOGEN is a CFD platform dedicated to the numerical simulation of compressible multiphase flows. It has the vocation to share academic research in the multiphase flow field with other academics, but also with industrials and students.

  • Multi-modelsSingle phase; multiphase with or without velocity, pressure, temperature and chemical-potential equilibria.
  • Multi-physicsThermal transfers, viscosity, surface tension, mass transfers, turbulence (LES).
  • Multi-meshesCartesian, unstructured, AMR.
  • HPC & I/OParallel computing with MPI, VTK and HDF5 (VTKHDF) outputs.

ECOGEN is Evolutive

Academic research results never represent achievements but rather steps forward. This is all the more noticeable in the young discipline of numerical sciences, where models and methods are in constant evolution to better represent physical phenomena. In the structure of ECOGEN, the modular aspect of the C++ programming language has been used to help developing future evolutions: it is easy for developers to add a new equation of state or a flow model.

ECOGEN is Compressible

Compressible information is transmitted by wave propagation. Mathematical systems of equations modelling compressible flows present hyperbolic formulations that should be specifically treated numerically. ECOGEN is designed for such compressible flows.

ECOGEN is Open source

ECOGEN is an open-source project distributed under the GNU GPLv3 license and available on GitHub. Interested developers are encouraged to contribute to the project.

ECOGEN is Genuine

Existing CFD codes can treat multiphase flows, but ECOGEN is the first to use the diffuse-interface theory to dynamically capture the complex shapes of multiphase flows on structured or unstructured grids. Moreover, it includes an Adaptive Mesh Refinement (AMR) method for Cartesian grids specifically developed and optimized for such multiphase flows, and equipped with efficient load balancing for multicore computations.

ECOGEN is Easy

All you need to use ECOGEN is a C++ compiler with a Message Passing Interface (MPI) library. The standard VTK file format used for results makes the post-processing flexible. Just follow the tutorials and compute!

ECOGEN is N-phase

Even if ECOGEN is able to solve single-phase gas or compressible liquid dynamics, it reveals its power when a flow is composed of an arbitrary number of phases. If you can simulate a liquid phase with its vapour, there is no difficulty to add one or several inert phases to the flow.

Features

  • Solves interface problems between pure or multicomponent fluids and mixtures of multiphase flows.
  • Treats surface tension, heat and mass transfers for cavitation and evaporating and condensing flows.
  • Computes wave propagation in strongly unsteady situations using a specific Adaptive Mesh Refinement.
  • Simulates complex geometries on unstructured grids.
  • Finite volume method, object-oriented C++, parallel computing with MPI.
