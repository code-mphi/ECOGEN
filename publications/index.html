Publications

Each part and specificity of ECOGEN has been validated and the results are published in international journals after a reviewing process: work in progress must pass this stage before being included in ECOGEN. Below are the known journal articles, PhD theses, conferences and preprints describing, improving or using ECOGEN (not simply citing it), most recent first.
